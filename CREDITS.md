# RUNEBOUND - credits for third-party assets

Everything in RUNEBOUND is made by its own scripts (`tools/`), except the
3D models listed here. Each of these models was downloaded from Sketchfab
under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes made to all of them: the mesh was cleaned and decimated, bent onto
RUNEBOUND's own skeleton and animations, and its painted texture was baked
onto a new pixel atlas and recoloured to the game's palette; glowing eyes or
seams were added. The original rigs and animations are not used.
